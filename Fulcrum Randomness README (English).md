# Fulcrum Randomness

A religious fulcrum and an Android app centered around randomness, choice, and karma.

## Features
- **Altar of Randomness** — spin options with adjustable speed and number of spins.
- **Secret Cells of Fate** — pick hidden cards by intuition.
- **Temple of Telepathic Prayers** — preset and custom prayers focused on randomness and good intentions.
- **Icon of Randomness and Karma** — reflections on randomness and karma, along with a chronicle of good deeds.
